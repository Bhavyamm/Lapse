# Review, Calendar screen (uncommitted), 2026-10-17

**Reviewed by**: Sonnet 5.5 (author model not stated)
**Scope**: 9 files, working tree (`git diff` plus untracked `calendar.ts`, `calendar.test.ts`, `calendar.test.tsx`)
**Verdict**: Changes requested

## Summary
The change replaces the Calendar placeholder with a month grid, a month summary in the header, prev/next buttons limited to this month through +11, and a "Coming up" / "First in {Month}" card, and extracts `monthCells` so the date picker shares the grid math. The date math holds up: charges are always computed from the anchor, so Jan 31 / Feb 28 / Mar 31 does not drift; Feb 29 yearly plans clamp and come back; DST is not a risk because everything goes through local `Date` constructors and `setDate`, never millisecond offsets; a charge before `from` is skipped but the loop still advances; and trial logic matches `overview.ts` (`sub.trial && first charge on or after today`). `tsc`, lint, prettier and all 117 tests pass. The real risks are accessibility (the day labels sit on role-less views, and every quiet day is hidden), a month view that silently jumps when today rolls over, and layouts that are fixed-size under large text. I read the code and did not run it on a device, so the screen reader and large text items are risks to confirm in Expo Go and on web.

## Major
### 🟠 Day and grid labels sit on views with no role, and quiet days are hidden, `src/app/(tabs)/calendar.tsx:117`, `:128-134`
**Problem**: The grid `View` has `aria-label` and each announced cell has `aria-label` but neither has a `role`. On react-native-web these render as generic `div`s, and `aria-label` on a generic element is not announced by most screen readers (and is prohibited by ARIA). On native, the label works, but the grid container is not an accessibility element, so it is never read either. Separately, every day with no charge is hidden with `aria-hidden` plus `importantForAccessibility="no-hide-descendants"`, so a VoiceOver or TalkBack user gets only the handful of days with charges and today, in a list that no longer says which day of the week or how far into the month they are (the weekday header is hidden too). The label for a day gives "Sat, Oct 17" with no year, which is ambiguous on months in next year.
**Why it matters**: On web the labels are lost entirely, and on native the grid is an unlabelled run of fragments. The `aria-hidden` plus `importantForAccessibility` pair is also redundant.
**Suggested fix**: Give announced cells `role="img"` (or render the label as hidden `Text` inside, which works everywhere) so the label is exposed on web. Give the grid `role="grid"`/no label and put the month name in the header, which is already the title, or wrap the grid in `role="group"` with the label. Keep quiet days hidden if that is the intent, but then add the year to `describeDay` when it differs from today's, and say "1 renewal" counts. Drop the `aria-hidden` on the cell and keep one mechanism (`importantForAccessibility` is native only, `aria-hidden` is both).

### 🟠 The month on screen jumps when today rolls over, `src/app/(tabs)/calendar.tsx:29-31`
**Problem**: State is `offset` from today's month, not an absolute month. `useToday()` refreshes at midnight and on foreground. If someone is looking at December (offset 2 in October) and the clock passes into November, `shiftMonth(today, 2)` becomes January: the screen flips a month with no user action. At the end of a month the same happens in the other direction for anyone who left the tab open. The comment says this "keeps the screen right", but it only keeps it right for offset 0. Also, if the person was on offset 11 and today rolls forward, they now see a month that was 12 ahead, which the buttons never allowed.
**Why it matters**: The visible month changes underneath someone reading it, and the `$X due in {Month}` line changes with it. Rare, but it is exactly the case the code comments claim to handle.
**Suggested fix**: Keep `{year, month}` (or a month index) in state and clamp it in render to `[currentMonth, currentMonth + MONTHS_AHEAD]` against the live `today`, so rollover moves the lower bound, not the view. Add a test that advances the clock across a month end while on a later month.

### 🟠 Fixed-size cells and the trial tag break at large text, `src/app/(tabs)/calendar.tsx:221-233`, `src/components/subscription-row.tsx:30-40`, `:78-92`
**Problem**: (a) Each cell is 44pt high with a 34pt circle, 6pt dot and 2pt gap (42pt). The day number is `label` (14pt with `lineHeight` 19) and scales with system text; at 150% to 200% a two digit day is wider and taller than the 34pt circle and spills onto the dot, and the weekday initials (`caption`, no height) grow into the first row. Nothing caps scaling (`maxFontSizeMultiplier`) or lets the cell grow. (b) In the compact row the tag has no `flexShrink`, so with long names and large text the name is truncated to nothing or the tag wraps into the price column. (c) The header row puts a title like "September 2027" and a 40pt pair of buttons side by side; the subtitle ("$1,234.56 still to pay this month") is `label` text that wraps fine, but the title does not shrink, and at large text it pushes the arrows.
**Why it matters**: People who raise text size get an overlapping grid, which is the one place a misread number matters (a renewal day).
**Suggested fix**: Use `minHeight` instead of `height` on the cell, size the circle with `minWidth`/`minHeight` plus `aspectRatio`, or cap the day number with `maxFontSizeMultiplier={1.3}` and let the labels carry the detail. Give the tag `flexShrink: 0` and the name `flex: 1` with `minWidth: 0`, and wrap the tag under the name at large sizes. Allow the header title `numberOfLines={1}` with `adjustsFontSizeToFit`. Verify on a 320pt phone at the largest setting.

## Minor
### 🟡 "One dot per renewal" is really one dot per day, and a trial day has no dot, `src/app/(tabs)/calendar.tsx:123-142`
**Problem**: `renews` is a boolean, so two renewals on the same day look like one, and a day with only a trial conversion shows the orange fill but no dot. The summary total includes both, so the grid and the "$X" do not visibly add up.
**Why it matters**: A day with three charges looks the same as a day with one. Contradicts the stated design intent.
**Suggested fix**: Render up to three dots (`charges.filter(...)` length, capped) or a small count beyond that, and keep the trial's fill as the extra signal. If one dot per day is the design, change the intent text.

### 🟡 "Still to pay" counts a charge that may already have gone out today, and the past of this month is blank, `src/features/subscriptions/calendar.ts:44-47`
**Problem**: In the current month `from` is today, inclusive, so a charge dated today is counted as still to pay even if the bank took it this morning. Charges earlier this month are removed from the total and also from the grid, so past days show no dots at all.
**Why it matters**: Mostly a copy and trust issue: "still to pay" with a charge that is arguably done, and a grid that looks as though nothing happened in the first half of the month.
**Suggested fix**: Decide and document it. Either keep today in and phrase it "$X left this month", or show past charges as faded dots (`past && styles.dotPast`) while still excluding them from the total. The second needs `planMonth` to return the full month's `byDay` plus a separate `dueCents`.

### 🟡 Iterating from the anchor on every render grows with the age of the anchor, `src/features/subscriptions/calendar.ts:35`
**Problem**: `chargesBetween` walks every cycle from `sub.chargeOn` to `to`, allocating a `Date` per step. A weekly plan entered with a date five years back is about 260 iterations per subscription per month change; ten years is 520. It is memoized per month, so it is fine now, but `nextChargeDate` has the same shape and the cost stacks with a long list.
**Why it matters**: Not a bug today; it will show up as a stutter when a person enters old dates for many weekly plans.
**Suggested fix**: Jump to the first cycle at or after `from` (for week: `Math.ceil(daysBetween(anchor, from) / 7)`; for month/year: derive from the year and month difference, then back off one if the clamped date is before `from`), then walk forward. Keep `count === 0` for the trial flag.

### 🟡 The list does not say when it is cut off, and "Coming up" is empty while next month is not, `src/app/(tabs)/calendar.tsx:153`, `:178`
**Problem**: Only the first 4 charges show with no "and N more" line, while the header total includes all of them. On the last days of a month with nothing left, the card says "No more charges this month." with the next month's renewals one tap away.
**Why it matters**: The total can look wrong against 4 visible rows.
**Suggested fix**: Add a caption row "+3 more this month" when `plan.charges.length > MONTH_LIST_LIMIT`, and for an empty current month consider pointing at the next month's first charge.

### 🟡 Month changes are not announced, `src/app/(tabs)/calendar.tsx:43-48`
**Problem**: The buttons are labelled "Previous month" / "Next month", but pressing one changes the title and subtitle with no live region or focus move, and the labels do not name the target month.
**Why it matters**: Screen reader users get no confirmation, and cannot tell which month "Previous" goes to.
**Suggested fix**: Label them with the target ("Show November") and add `aria-live="polite"` on the header text, or move focus to the title after a press. The disabled state (`aria-disabled` plus `disabled`) is correct.

### 🟡 Hard-coded sizes instead of tokens, `src/app/(tabs)/calendar.tsx:191-253`
**Problem**: Arrow size 40, cell height 44, circle 34, dot 6, paddings 14 and 10, and a hard-coded `new Date(2026, 1, 1 + i)` for weekday names. AGENTS.md says not to hard-code new colors or font sizes; the colors and fonts here are tokens, but spacing is partly literal.
**Why it matters**: Small drift from the design system; the weekday trick depends on 1 Feb 2026 being a Sunday and is repeated at every render.
**Suggested fix**: Pull the sizes into named constants at the top of the file, and compute the weekday list once at module scope. Use the `Pressable` `minHeight`/`minWidth` of 44 from a shared token if one exists.

### 🟡 Compact rows lose the billing cycle and nothing is tappable, `src/components/subscription-row.tsx:28-40`
**Problem**: In compact mode only the name and a "Trial ends" tag show; a $120 charge on a yearly plan reads the same as a $120 monthly one. The row also has no press target, but the card sits under the calendar where people will try to tap a charge.
**Why it matters**: The cycle is useful context for a number that big, and a non-interactive row in a tab that looks interactive is a repeat of the Home trial card finding.
**Suggested fix**: Keep the cycle in the right-hand caption ("Sat, Oct 24 · Yearly") or as a second line when the font is large. Link the row to a detail screen when one exists.

### 🟡 Tests miss the risky branches, `src/features/subscriptions/calendar.test.ts`, `src/__tests__/calendar.test.tsx`
**Problem**: Present and good: cycles, month-end, trial flag, current vs later month, today inclusive, year rollover, navigation limits and the empty state. Missing: a weekly plan across a month boundary, a yearly Feb 29 anchor, an anchor many years back (and its cost), a trial whose date has passed (becomes a normal renewal, no orange), two charges on one day, today rolling into a new month while on a later one (see Major), the aria labels in `describeDay`, and `monthCells` for a month starting on Saturday (six rows).
**Why it matters**: These are the branches where the dots or the total go wrong without a failing test.
**Suggested fix**: Add the cases above; assert the `aria-label` text of a trial day and of today.

## Resolution

Fixed:
- Major, accessibility: the grid now uses `role="grid"`, `"row"` and `"cell"`, with the year in the grid's label. Every day is announced with its charges (checked on web: one grid, five rows, labelled cells). The redundant hiding props are gone and the weekday row is hidden on Android too.
- Major, rollover: the screen keeps the month you picked as an absolute month and clamps it to this month through 11 ahead. A day change no longer moves what you're looking at.
- Major, large text: day cells and circles use minimum sizes and the day digits are capped at 1.3x so the grid keeps seven columns. The "Trial ends" tag no longer shrinks; the name truncates first.
- The list says "N more this month" or "N more in November" when it leaves charges out.
- Tests for the more line and the grid label.

Deferred:
- One dot per day, and no dot on a trial-only day: this matches the design.
- A charge dated today counts as still to pay. Without a bank link the app can't know it went out. Revisit when a charge can be marked paid.
- `chargesBetween` walking from the anchor: cheap at real sizes. Revisit when the SQLite data layer lands.
- Tapping a row opens Subscription detail once that screen exists.
- Month-change announcements and arrow labels naming the target month: in the accessibility pass.
