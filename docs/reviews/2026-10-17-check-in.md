# Review, Monthly check-in screen (commit 49321c9), 2026-10-17

**Reviewed by**: Sonnet 5.5 (author model: Opus 5.5)
**Scope**: 21 files, `git show HEAD` (check-in screen, `check-in.ts` queue and `isDue`, Home "Monthly check-in" row, Profile row, `checkin` reminder in `plan.ts` and its tap handling, `yearlyCents`, BigAmount `light`, `reviewedOn`)
**Verdict**: Changes requested

## Summary
The structure is sound: the queue is a pure function and is frozen when the screen opens, so keeping one does not reshuffle the rest; a subscription cancelled from its detail screen drops out of the queue and is counted in the summary; `addedOn` converts the UTC `createdAt` to the local day, so a subscription added late on the 31st counts as added that day; the reminder dates are built from local `new Date(y, m + n, 1, 9)`, which handles year end and DST; running trials are excluded consistently in the queue, the Home count and the reminder count; the screen is only mounted after both stores hydrate. `tsc`, lint and all 225 tests pass. The real problems are: "Next month" does not remember anything, so the Home row and the check-in never clear for a skipped subscription, the reminder skips the 1st when the app is opened on the 1st before 9 AM, and the summary calls a subscription "cancelled" for any reason it disappears. I read the code and did not run it on a device.

## Critical
None.

## Major
### 🟠 "Next month" does not defer anything, `src/app/check-in.tsx:42-46`, `src/features/subscriptions/check-in.ts:23`
**Problem**: `answer('later')` only writes to the screen's `outcomes` state; the store is not touched (the test even asserts `yt.reviewedOn` stays `2026-09-16`). `isDue` stays true for that subscription for the rest of the month, so (1) the Home row keeps saying "1 subscription to look over" after the person finished the check-in and was told "See you next month", and (2) opening the check-in again from Home or Profile asks about it again, with the "left 1 for next month" summary now untrue. The only way to clear the row is to Keep or cancel it, which turns "Next month" into a button that does nothing durable.
**Why it matters**: A permanent nag on the Home screen for exactly the people who use the skip, and a button whose label promises something the app does not do.
**Suggested fix**: Persist the skip, for example a `skippedOn` (or `reviewSnoozedUntil`) local date set to `today` and treated as "not due this month" in `isDue`/`checkInQueue` (it must not change the "Last reviewed" pill, which should still show the last Keep). Add tests: skip, reopen the screen, and assert it is not in the queue; skip on Oct 16, and assert it is due again on Nov 1; assert `dueCount` drops.

### 🟠 No reminder for the 1st when the app is opened on the 1st before 9 AM, `src/features/reminders/plan.ts:147-150`
**Problem**: The loop starts at `month = 1`, i.e. next month, always. On Nov 1 at 08:00 (a very common time for a first app open, or any reschedule triggered by editing a subscription), the 9 AM reminder for that same day is never planned, and the next one is Dec 1. The planner already knows how to skip times in the past (`firstReminderTime` checks `at > now`), but this loop does not consider month 0.
**Why it matters**: The reminder is the only thing that brings people back for the check-in, and it is lost on the exact day it is for whenever the schedule is rebuilt that morning.
**Suggested fix**: Start at `month = 0` and `continue` when `first.getTime() <= now.getTime()`. Add a test with `now` = Nov 1 08:00 expecting `checkin:2026-11-01` first, and one with `now` = Nov 1 09:30 expecting it absent.

## Minor
### 🟡 The summary calls every missing subscription "cancelled", `src/app/check-in.tsx:94`, `:158`
**Problem**: `gone` is "in the queue but no longer in the store". Anything that removes a subscription counts, not only `cancel` (the store has `remove`, and a future delete or a sync would use it). It is also computed from ids, not from `cancelled`, so the number is not checked against what really went into `cancelled`.
**Why it matters**: The summary can state a cancellation (and a savings story) that did not happen.
**Suggested fix**: Count `ids.filter(id => cancelled.some(c => c.id === id))` using the `cancelled` list from the store; ignore ids that are simply gone. Test with `remove('gpt')` and assert the summary does not say "cancelled".

### 🟡 Nothing tells a screen-reader user the card changed, `src/app/check-in.tsx:68`, `:113`
**Problem**: After Keep it / Next month, focus stays on the action button and the card content is swapped in place. The new subscription (name, amount, "2 of 5") is never announced, so VoiceOver/TalkBack users answer blind until they swipe back up. The same goes for the move to "All checked".
**Why it matters**: The screen is a loop of one decision; the thing being decided on is silent.
**Suggested fix**: Give the card (and the finished title) `aria-live="polite"`, or move accessibility focus to the card after each answer. "Cancel it" also only opens the cancel steps; consider `aria-label="Cancel it, opens the steps"` so the label matches what happens.

### 🟡 The reminder text is frozen with a count that can be wrong, `src/features/reminders/plan.ts:143-155`
**Problem**: `count` is the number of non-trial subscriptions today, including ones with `remind: false`, written into the body of notifications that fire up to 90 days later. A person who cancels three subscriptions still gets "Take a minute to see if your 5 subscriptions are still worth it" until the schedule is rebuilt, and one who has a single subscription added this month is told to check a subscription that is not due.
**Why it matters**: Wrong numbers in a notification look like a bug.
**Suggested fix**: Drop the number from the body ("Take a minute to see which subscriptions are still worth it."), or count only what will be due. Test both bodies.

### 🟡 Tapping the reminder while the check-in is open stacks a second one, `src/components/reminders.tsx:70-72`
**Problem**: `router.push('/check-in')` always pushes. With the check-in open (or already on top of a detail screen), a tap adds another instance with a fresh queue and `outcomes`, and Back returns to the old one, which has since been changed by the store.
**Why it matters**: Confusing double progress ("1 of 4" after finishing 3).
**Suggested fix**: Use `router.navigate('/check-in')` (or `dismissTo`) so an existing instance is reused.

## Nits
- `src/features/subscriptions/check-in.ts:23`: a subscription kept on Oct 31 is due again on Nov 1; "once a month" reads more like "once per calendar month". Fine if intended, but worth saying in the copy.
- `src/app/check-in.tsx:39`: if a subscription is edited into a running trial from the detail screen, it stays in the frozen queue and Keep sets `reviewedOn` on a trial; harmless, but skip trials in `current` too.
- `src/features/reminders/plan.test.ts:142-160`: no test for the 1st being today, for Dec to Jan beyond the one 3-month case, or for the 60 cap keeping check-ins ahead of renewals.
- `src/features/subscriptions/check-in.test.ts`: no test of `addedOn` near midnight in a non-UTC zone (set `TZ` and a `createdAt` that is already the next UTC day).

## Resolution

Fixed:
- Major, "Next month" deferred nothing: it now saves `skippedOn`, and `isDue` takes the latest of added, kept and put off, so the Home row clears and the subscription comes back next month.
- Major, no reminder on the 1st before 9 AM: the planner starts from this month's 1st and skips it only once its time has passed.
- The summary counts as cancelled only subscriptions that are in the `cancelled` records; a deleted one isn't counted.
- "Cancel it" carries a hint that it shows the cancel steps.
- The reminder body no longer states a count that could be stale.
- Tapping the reminder navigates to the check-in instead of pushing a second one.
- Tests for each: 227 passing.

Deferred:
- Announcing the card change to screen readers, and keeping a trial that was edited back into a running trial out of an open check-in: in the accessibility and polish pass.
- Kept on Oct 31, due again Nov 1: that's the monthly rhythm by design.
