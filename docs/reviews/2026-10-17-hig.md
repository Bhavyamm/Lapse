# Review, Human Interface Guidelines audit (iOS), 2026-10-17

**Reviewed by**: Opus 5.5, against the HIG skills in `.claude/skills/hig-*` and `.claude/apple-design-context.md`
**Scope**: every screen in `src/app` (tabs, Add, subscription detail, Trial, check-in, Pro, onboarding reminders), the shared components in `src/components`, `src/theme/tokens.ts`, notification content in `src/features/reminders`
**Verdict**: Changes requested

## Summary
The brand look (charcoal, orange, Bricolage Grotesque, floating nav) is intentional and is not questioned here; the findings are places where it breaks an iOS convention people rely on. Much is already right: Reduce Motion is honoured everywhere I checked (`PressScale` dims instead of scaling, `BottomSheet`, `Toggle`, `Segmented`, `Appear`, `animateLayout`, the check-in swipe and celebration, the splash fade), controls use `role`/`aria-*`, most tap targets reach 44 pt through size or `hitSlop`, modals have a visible Close, and the Face ID usage string says why. The main text pairs pass WCAG AA: ink on orange 6.56:1, #555555 on canvas 5.94:1 (7.46:1 on white), #B5B5B5 on charcoal 7.66:1 (5.98:1 on the `onDarkPanel` tiles), #8A8A8A on the splash's night 5.24:1, orange on charcoal 5.6:1. White on orange would be 2.81:1, and the app never puts white text on orange, so keep it that way. What doesn't work: fixed heights around text break larger Dynamic Type sizes, a 1.8 s branded splash runs on every launch, closing the editor sheet or Add throws away edits without asking, nothing plays haptics, and the custom sheet, switch and tab bar each drop part of what the system version provides. I read the code and computed sizes and contrast from the styles; I did not run it on a device.

## Critical
None.

## Major
### 🟠 Every primary button has a fixed 60 pt height, so larger text overflows it, `src/components/pill-button.tsx:46`, `src/app/check-in.tsx:610`
**HIG**: `hig-foundations/references/typography.md`, "Supporting Dynamic Type: Make sure your app's layout adapts to all font sizes"; `hig-foundations/references/layout.md`, "Be prepared for text-size changes".
**Problem**: `PillButton` is `height: 60` with a 46 pt knob, and the label has no room to wrap. It is the main action on the lock screen ("Unlock with Face ID"), onboarding ("Turn on reminders"), Add ("Add 3 and remind me"), the editor ("Save"), Trial ("Cancel before Saturday") and Home's empty state. At AX1 (about 1.65x) the 17 pt bold label is about 28 pt. "Cancel before Wednesday" or "Add 3 and remind me" then wraps to two 36 pt lines (72 pt) inside a 60 pt pill, and the text spills past the pill's edge. The check-in "Done" button is `height: 52` too.
**Why it matters**: People who use large text can't read the one button on each screen, including the button that unlocks the app.
**Suggested fix**: Make `button` `minHeight: 60` with `paddingVertical: 10`. Give the label `flexShrink: 1` and a `marginRight` so it wraps beside the knob instead of under it. Change the check-in Done button to `minHeight: 52`. Add a test that renders `PillButton` at `fontScale` 2 and checks the style has no fixed `height`.

### 🟠 Fixed-height rows, fields and cards clip larger text, and key labels are cut to one line, `src/app/add.tsx:312`, `:339`, `:360`, `src/components/subscription-editor.tsx:220`, `src/components/chip.tsx:34`, `src/components/date-picker.tsx:146`, `:152`, `src/app/check-in.tsx:464-470`, `src/app/subscription/[id].tsx:182`, `:265`
**HIG**: `hig-foundations/references/typography.md`, "Make sure your app's layout adapts to all font sizes" and "Keep text truncation to a minimum as font size increases".
**Problem**: (1) The Add catalog rows are `height: 64` and hold two lines (21 + 18 pt at 1x). They are full at about 1.65x and clip above that. The search field and the price field are `height: 52`, and "Add your own" is `height: 54`. Chips are `height: 34`. Date picker cells are `height: 44` around a 38 pt circle, with no `maxFontSizeMultiplier`, unlike the Calendar tab (`calendar.tsx:131`, `:156`). (2) The check-in card sits in an absolutely positioned slot inside a stack capped at `maxHeight: 380`. Its content (64 pt logo, 31 pt title, 64 pt amount, about 4 lede lines) is already about 300 pt at 1x on a 270 to 350 pt slot. At larger sizes the text overflows the white card onto the buttons below. (3) The detail screen's main action is `numberOfLines={1}`. "Search how to cancel Disney+ Hotstar Premium" is cut even at default size. The three hero facts are also one line each in a third of the width, so "$1,199.88" truncates at larger sizes. These fixed heights were already flagged in `2026-10-17-add-subscription.md` and are still open.
**Why it matters**: The Add list, the price field and the cancel action are the core flow. At accessibility sizes they overlap or hide the text that says what they do.
**Suggested fix**: Change each `height` to `minHeight` with vertical padding (rows `minHeight: 64, paddingVertical: 10`; fields `minHeight: 52, paddingVertical: 12`; chips `minHeight: 34, paddingVertical: 6`). Give date picker days and weekday letters `maxFontSizeMultiplier={1.3}`, as on the Calendar tab. Wrap the check-in `ReviewCard` content in a `ScrollView`. Or, once `fontScale > 1.3` (from `useWindowDimensions().fontScale`), drop the stack's `maxHeight` and switch the card to normal layout. Change the primary action to `numberOfLines={2}`. Let the hero facts wrap to two rows when `fontScale > 1.3`.

### 🟠 A 1.8 s branded splash plays on every launch, `src/components/launch-splash.tsx:14`, `:34`, `src/app/_layout.tsx:38`
**HIG**: `hig-patterns/references/launching.md`, "Launch instantly", "If you need a splash screen, consider displaying it at the beginning of your onboarding flow", "Downplay the launch experience", "Don't advertise"; `hig-patterns/references/onboarding.md`, "Briefly display a splash screen if necessary".
**Problem**: `splashing` starts `true` on every cold launch. `LaunchSplash` then holds the screen for `SPLASH_MS = 1800` plus a 250 ms fade, after fonts and stores have loaded. The splash has a mascot, a tagline and a speech bubble, and the only hint that a tap skips it is in the VoiceOver label. With VoiceOver on, it waits for a tap with no time limit. This was a deliberate design choice (`2026-10-17-home-list-splash.md`), but it runs against the guideline on every launch, not only the first.
**Why it matters**: lapse is a check-and-go utility. Opened from a reminder or to glance at what's due, it costs 2 s before anything useful appears, every time.
**Suggested fix**: Show the branded splash only on first run, as the start of onboarding (`!hasOnboarded`). On later launches go straight from the native splash (already #1C1C1C with the logo, from `app.json`) to the app, with at most a short fade. If the brand moment must stay, cut it to about 600 ms and skip it when the app opens from a notification response. With VoiceOver on, announce the tagline and continue on its own instead of waiting for a tap.

### 🟠 Closing the editor or leaving Add throws away work without asking, `src/components/bottom-sheet.tsx:58`, `src/components/subscription-editor.tsx:101`, `src/app/add.tsx:82`, `:99`
**HIG**: `hig-patterns/references/modality.md`, "When necessary, help people avoid data loss by getting confirmation before closing a modal view"; `hig-components-dialogs/references/action-sheets.md` (a discard confirmation is the standard pattern).
**Problem**: In `SubscriptionEditor`, a tap on the dimmed backdrop (`onPress={onClose}`) or on Close drops the typed name, price, cycle, trial and date at once. A stray tap above a tall sheet is easy, because the sheet is up to 92% of the screen. On Add, Back (and the iOS edge swipe back, since `add` is a card in the stack) leaves with all picks discarded. Someone who picked six services and adjusted two loses all of it.
**Why it matters**: These two screens are where people enter data, and re-entering prices is tedious.
**Suggested fix**: Track whether the draft differs from `initial`. While it does, make the backdrop do nothing. On Close, show a confirmation with "Discard changes" (`style: 'destructive'`) and "Keep editing" (`style: 'cancel'`). Extend `confirm` in `src/utils/notify.ts` to take a destructive flag. On Add, when `drafts.length > 0`, ask "Discard 3 picks?" before leaving. Block the swipe back with React Navigation's `usePreventRemove` (check how SDK 57's Expo Router exposes it before relying on it).

## Minor
### 🟡 No haptic feedback on any action; `expo-haptics` is available in Expo Go but not installed, `package.json`, `src/app/add.tsx:83`, `src/app/check-in.tsx:59`, `src/app/subscription/[id].tsx:87`, `src/app/pro.tsx:35`, `src/components/toggle.tsx`, `src/components/chip.tsx`, `src/components/segmented.tsx`
**HIG**: `hig-patterns/references/playing-haptics.md`, "Use system-provided haptic patterns according to their documented meanings", "Prefer using haptics to complement other feedback", "In most apps, prefer playing short haptics that complement discrete events", and under iOS: standard controls such as toggles play haptics themselves.
**Problem**: `node_modules/expo/bundledNativeModules.json` lists `expo-haptics` `~57.0.3`, so it ships in Expo Go 57, but it isn't a dependency, and nothing in `src` plays a haptic. The custom `Toggle`, `Chip`, `Segmented` and date picker replace system controls that would tap on change. The milestone actions are silent: saving on Add, Keep it / Next month in the check-in, Mark cancelled, Pro unlocked, and the editor's validation error.
**Why it matters**: The custom controls feel inert next to system ones, and the check-in (a rapid series of decisions) has no physical confirmation.
**Suggested fix**: Run `npx expo install expo-haptics`. Add `src/utils/haptics.ts` with `selection()`, `success()`, `warning()` and `error()` wrappers that do nothing on web and swallow errors. Play `selectionAsync` on toggle, chip, segment and date changes; `impactAsync(Light)` on check-in Keep it / Next month; `notificationAsync(Success)` after Add saves, Mark cancelled confirms and Pro unlocks; `notificationAsync(Error)` when `draftProblem` blocks Save. Don't add them to scrolling or every `PressScale` press ("Avoid overusing haptics"). Mock `expo-haptics` in `jest.setup`.

### 🟡 Bottom sheets have no grabber and can't be swiped down, `src/components/bottom-sheet.tsx:55-67`
**HIG**: `hig-components-dialogs/references/sheets.md`, "Support swiping to dismiss a sheet", "Include a grabber in a resizable sheet", "In an iPhone app, consider supporting the medium detent".
**Problem**: `BottomSheet` is a transparent `Modal` with a sliding `Animated.View`. It closes only from the Close button, the backdrop or Android back. Swiping down, the gesture iOS users try first on any sheet, does nothing, and there's no grabber to suggest it. The editor sheet is tall (up to 92%), so the backdrop is a thin strip.
**Why it matters**: Every system sheet in iOS dismisses with a downward swipe, so this one feels stuck.
**Suggested fix**: Add a 36 x 5 pt grabber (`colors.hairline`, radius 3) centred at the top of both sheets. Wrap the sheet in a `GestureDetector` with a `Gesture.Pan()` from `react-native-gesture-handler` (already installed and in Expo Go). Follow the finger downward, and on release past 30% of the sheet's height (or a fast fling) call `onClose`; otherwise spring back with `SETTLE`. Route the swipe through the same unsaved-changes check as Close (see the Major above), and skip the follow animation with Reduce Motion.

### 🟡 The switch's off state is nearly invisible on white, `src/components/toggle.tsx:36`
**HIG**: `hig-components-controls/references/toggles.md`, "Make sure the visual differences in a toggle's state are obvious"; `hig-foundations/references/accessibility.md`, "Strive to meet color contrast minimum standards".
**Problem**: Off is a `colors.hairline` (#D6D6D6) track with a white knob. On the white Reminders and editor sheets that track is 1.45:1 against its background, and the white knob nearly disappears against the white sheet. On canvas-coloured surfaces it is 1.16:1. WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for the parts that identify a control's state. It affects "Remind me", "Free trial", "Face ID lock" and "Renewal alert".
**Why it matters**: Off reads as a faint outline, so people can't tell at a glance whether reminders are on, the app's core setting.
**Suggested fix**: Add a `controlOff` token, e.g. `#8E8E93` (3.26:1 on white), for the off track. Or keep the light fill and add a 1.5 pt `colors.inkSoft` border to the track when off. Check the result on the glass cards too.

### 🟡 Two text colours fall under 4.5:1, `src/components/subscription-editor.tsx:248`, `src/app/add.tsx:102`, `src/components/screen-background.tsx:31`
**HIG**: `hig-foundations/references/accessibility.md`, "Strive to meet color contrast minimum standards"; `hig-foundations/references/color.md`.
**Problem**: (1) `colors.danger` (#E23B4A) on the white editor sheet is 4.24:1. The validation message is 14 pt semibold, which is not large text, so it needs 4.5:1. (2) `ScreenBackground` puts a 50% orange glow in the top-right corner. `textSecondary` (#555555) over the glow's peak is about 3.9:1 (#EAB090 behind it), and Add's "N services" caption (13 pt) sits right there. Elsewhere on canvas the same colour is 5.94:1.
**Why it matters**: The error message is the only explanation of why Save did nothing. Muted captions near the glow are the hardest text in the app to read in sunlight.
**Suggested fix**: Darken `danger` to `#C62E3C` (5.44:1 on white; it stays clearly red). Render the Add count in `colors.ink` with the medium weight, or lower the warm glow's opacity to about 0.3, which keeps #555555 above 4.5:1 everywhere on it.

### 🟡 Two controls are under 44 x 44 pt, `src/app/add.tsx:265-271`, `:409-416`, `src/components/reminder-sheet.tsx:76`
**HIG**: `hig-foundations/references/accessibility.md`, "Offer sufficiently sized controls" and "Consider spacing between controls as important as size" (Apple's minimum is 44 x 44 pt).
**Problem**: The remove "x" on each pick in Add's tray is 24 x 24 with `hitSlop={8}`, which gives 40 x 40, right next to the pick's own "Change" button. "Open Settings" in the Reminders sheet is a 14 pt link (19 pt line) with `hitSlop={8}`, about 35 pt tall. Everything else I measured reaches 44 pt (tabs 48, + 48, Close 44 or 36 + 16 slop, segments and chips through slop, date cells about 49 x 44).
**Why it matters**: The remove target is small and next to another target, so a mis-tap opens the editor or removes the wrong pick. "Open Settings" is the only way out when notifications are blocked.
**Suggested fix**: Give `pickRemove` `hitSlop={10}` (44 pt) and add `marginLeft: 2` so it doesn't overlap `pickMain`'s area, or draw it at 28 pt with `hitSlop={8}`. Give the Open Settings `Pressable` `minHeight: 44` and `justifyContent: 'center'`.

### 🟡 The tab bar has no labels, its icons don't scale, and re-tapping a tab does nothing, `src/components/floating-tab-bar.tsx:27`, `:30-38`, `:54`
**HIG**: `hig-components-layout/references/tab-bars.md`, "Include tab labels to help with navigation", "Consider using SF Symbols to provide familiar, scalable tab bar icons"; `hig-foundations/references/typography.md`, "Increase the size of meaningful interface icons as font size increases".
**Problem**: The floating pill shows four icon-only tabs. "Home", "Calendar", "All subscriptions" and "Profile" exist only as `aria-label`, so a sighted person has to guess what the list icon and the profile icon mean. The icons stay the same size at any text size. A custom bar also loses the system tab bar's Large Content Viewer (long press at accessibility sizes). Tapping the tab you're on is ignored (`if (!focused ...)`), where iOS scrolls to the top.
**Why it matters**: Navigation is the first thing a new user has to learn, and the icons alone don't teach it.
**Suggested fix**: Keep the pill, but show the selected tab's title beside its icon inside the orange capsule (an expanding pill, `type.label`, `numberOfLines={1}`), and let the bar's width follow its content instead of `tabBar.width`. Scale the icons with `Math.min(fontScale, 1.4)`. On a re-tap of the focused tab, emit `tabPress` and scroll the screen's `ScrollView` to the top (`useScrollToTop` from React Navigation with a ref in `TabScreen`).

### 🟡 The "Cancel the trial" notification action doesn't cancel anything, and the other actions end in a blocking alert, `src/features/reminders/sync.ts:46-52`, `src/components/reminders.tsx:91`, `:97`, `src/features/reminders/plan.ts:72`
**HIG**: `hig-components-system/references/notifications.md`, "Provide beneficial actions that make sense in the context of your notification", "Avoid providing an action that merely opens your app", "Write succinct, easy-to-read notification content"; `hig-components-menus/references/buttons.md`, "Ensure that each button clearly communicates its purpose".
**Problem**: The trial reminder's first button says "Cancel the trial". It opens the subscription's how-to-cancel screen; lapse can't cancel anything itself. "Remind me tonight" and "I'll keep it" both bring lapse to the foreground (the comment says this is so the choice is handled when the app wasn't running). Then they stop the person with an alert ("Okay" / "Got it") they have to dismiss before doing anything. The renewal body repeats the title: "Netflix renews tomorrow" / "$15.49 a month, renews Oct 25".
**Why it matters**: A button that claims to cancel a paid trial and doesn't is the worst kind of surprise for this app. The alerts add a tap to a choice that was already made.
**Suggested fix**: Rename the action "How to cancel" (or "See how to cancel"). After "Remind me tonight" and "I'll keep it", drop the alert and let the Home trial card show the change. Or test `opensAppToForeground: false` on those two actions in a development build. Check the SDK 57 `expo-notifications` docs first: the response must still reach the app when it is not running. Change the renewal body to something like "$15.49 a month, charged Oct 25."

### 🟡 The notification pre-prompt has two skip buttons and claims three steps, `src/app/reminders.tsx:54-59`, `:101-105`
**HIG**: `hig-foundations/references/privacy.md`, "Pre-alert screens: Include only one button and make it clear that it opens the system alert" and "Don't include additional actions in your custom screen"; `hig-patterns/references/onboarding.md`, "design a brief, enjoyable experience".
**Problem**: The screen that leads to the iOS notification prompt has "Turn on reminders", "Not now" at the bottom and "Skip" at the top right, and both skips do the same thing. `PageDots count={3} current={1}` (spoken as "Step 2 of 3") while this is the only onboarding screen; the welcome screen became the launch splash. The timing chips are fine: they are part of the request, not a separate action.
**Why it matters**: A person expecting two more steps doesn't get them, and two ways out next to the main button weaken the request this screen exists for.
**Suggested fix**: Remove the top-right "Skip" and keep "Not now" under the button. Remove `PageDots` (or pass the real count, 1, which draws nothing useful). Optional: since the button label already says the system will ask, consider moving the request to the first "Add and remind me", when the value is concrete.

### 🟡 Display-size numbers grow without limit, `src/app/trial/[id].tsx:91`, `:100`, `src/components/big-amount.tsx:24`, `src/app/pro.tsx:98`
**HIG**: `hig-foundations/references/typography.md`, "Consider adjusting your layout at large font sizes" and "Maintain a consistent information hierarchy regardless of the current font size".
**Problem**: `countdown` is 114 pt, `display` 60 pt and `wordmark` 52 pt, and all scale with the system setting. At AX5 (about 3x) the Trial countdown is about 340 pt tall and the word "Today" is wider than the screen. Home's "$1,234" at 180 pt spills off the spend card. These are already larger than any Dynamic Type size, so scaling them adds no legibility. Item (a) of `2026-10-17-home-list-splash.md` covered the amount and is still open.
**Why it matters**: At large sizes these hero numbers push everything else off screen or clip, and the hierarchy inverts.
**Suggested fix**: Add a `maxScale` per variant in `tokens.ts` (`countdown`, `display`, `wordmark`, `hero`, `amountUnit` about 1.3) and apply it as `maxFontSizeMultiplier` in `AppText`. Also give the dollars `numberOfLines={1}` and `adjustsFontSizeToFit`, so a large amount shrinks to fit the card.

## Resolution
All checks pass (typecheck, lint, format, 266 tests). Verified on the web build with Playwright: the tab bar capsule, the editor sheet with its grabber, and the discard questions on Close and Back (`Discard changes?`, `Discard 2 picks?`, Back stays on Add when declined).

- **Fixed-height buttons**: `PillButton` uses `minHeight: 60` with vertical padding and a shrinking label; check-in Done uses `minHeight: 52`. Test: `pill-button.test.tsx`.
- **Fixed-height rows and fields**: Add rows, search, "Add your own", the price field, chips and date picker cells use `minHeight`; date picker days and weekdays cap at 1.3x like the Calendar tab. The detail action wraps to two lines and the hero facts stack when `fontScale > 1.3`. The check-in card's content is a `ScrollView` that scrolls only above 1.3x.
- **Splash on every launch**: not changed. Showing the splash on each open was Bhavya's design choice (`2026-10-17-home-list-splash.md`); flagged to Bhavya instead.
- **Data loss**: the editor tracks whether the draft changed. Close, the backdrop, a swipe down and Android back ask "Discard changes?" (`Discard changes` destructive, `Keep editing` cancel). Add asks "Discard N picks?" from Back, and `usePreventRemove` (from `expo-router/react-navigation`) sends the iOS edge swipe and Android back through the same question. `confirm` takes `cancel` and `destructive` options. Tests in `add.test.tsx`.
- **Haptics**: installed `expo-haptics` ~57.0.3 (in Expo Go). `src/utils/haptics.ts` wraps selection, light impact, success and error, skipping the web and swallowing failures. Played on Toggle changes, Chip, Segmented and date picks, check-in answers, Add saving, Mark cancelled, Pro unlock and a blocked Save. Not on every press. Tests: `haptics.test.ts`, plus assertions in the Add and check-in tests.
- **Sheet grabber and swipe**: `BottomSheet` draws a 36 x 5 grabber and follows a downward pull (PanResponder, so scroll views inside keep their scrolling). Past 30% of its height or a fast fling it asks `onClose`, which can return false to keep it open; then it springs back.
- **Switch off state**: new `controlOff` token #8E8E93 (3.3:1 on white).
- **Contrast**: `danger` is #C62E3C (5.4:1 on white). Add's count is ink, not muted.
- **Tap targets**: the pick remove button is 28 pt + 8 slop (44); Open Settings has `minHeight: 44`.
- **Tab bar**: the open tab shows its label in the orange capsule ("Subscriptions" via `tabBarLabel`), the bar widens to fit, and re-tapping the open tab scrolls to the top (`useScrollToTop` in `TabScreen`). Icons stay at 1x; the bar caps its label at 1.2x. Test: `floating-tab-bar.test.tsx`.
- **Notifications**: the action is "How to cancel" and the renewal body is "$15.49 a month, charged Oct 25." The foreground alerts after Remind me tonight / I'll keep it are unchanged; dropping them needs a development build check of background actions, noted for later.
- **Onboarding pre-prompt**: removed the top Skip and the page dots (and the unused `PageDots` component).
- **Display sizes**: `maxScale` in `tokens.ts` caps wordmark, display, hero, countdown and amountUnit at 1.3x through `AppText`.
- **Nits**: check-in Cancel it uses `ArrowUpRightIcon`; Mark cancelled offers "Keep tracking"; Profile says "Trials caught" for 0 or 2+; `useReduceMotion` starts from the value read at launch. The Calendar legend and the detail tile switch are left as they are.
